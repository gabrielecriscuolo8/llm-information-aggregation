Interpretation boundary

This pilot concerns one model, synthetic conditionally independent signals, and fixed prompts. It does not establish performance on real news or across model families. The market is promising only if it beats both ensemble aggregation rules and the central analyst, or establishes a defensible accuracy/cost advantage.
